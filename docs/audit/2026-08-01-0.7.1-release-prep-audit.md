## Known limitations

- The 16 XTCP pairwise scenarios are CI/VPS-only and were not run on the local machine.
- `cargo audit` ignores are documented in `deny.toml`: `rsa 0.10.0-rc.18` (no fixed release, verification-only use) and `quick-xml 0.26.x` (profiling-only developer path, not reachable from network input or shipped builds).
- Criterion reported variance/regression labels on some micro-benchmarks under high local machine load; the performance audit found no structural hot-path regression in the fix wave.
